# State of the MindWalking work — 2026-08-01

A snapshot of what was built from the two source manuals, what it measures, and what is still
wrong. Written at the end of an unattended session; nothing here is polished away.

The three design documents remain the reference: `mindwalking-domain.md` (the method),
`phenomena-detection-design.md` (the detector, with an "as built" section recording where the
design was wrong), `session-narrative-design.md` (the protocol parser).

---

## What exists now

Stages 1–4 of the detection roadmap are implemented and exercised by 159 backend tests and
6 Playwright E2E tests.

**L0 — conditioning** (`services/phenomena/conditioning.py`). Resolves any GSR export to a
continuous LP channel: `Data(16 bit)` anchored on `Baseline` → `Resistance` fitted against
`Baseline` → `Resistance` with nominal constants → `Conductance` → bare `Baseline`. Mirrored in
`frontend/src/utils/gsrParser.ts`, with `tests/fixtures/mindwalker_export.csv` asserted from both
sides so the two cannot drift again.

**L0 — artefacts** (`detectors/artefact.py`). Rails, session-relative excessive movement,
electrode settling. Movement spans are reported as `KB` rather than silently dropped, because the
manual requires a Körperbewegung to be marked.

**L1 — primitives** (`primitives.py`). Tonic/phasic split, hysteresis leg segmentation with stall
timeout and onset/peak trimming, robust noise scale.

**L2 — detectors.** `A`, `T` (`deflection.py`), `BE`, `LPA_slow` (`discharge.py`), `LPB`, `LPD`
(`level.py`).

**L3 — cross-modal** (`detectors/stimulus.py`, `services/protocol.py`). BK3 cue inventory with
solo phrasings and observed variants, turn segmentation, function roles, the procedure/exercise
parse tree, stimulus locking, `X`, `KVZ`.

**Calibration** (`calibration.py`). Optional `lp_offset` and `a_unit_lp` on the analyse request.
Without them no charge zone is named and every A-magnitude is flagged uncalibrated.

**UI** (`components/PhenomenaPanel.tsx`). Ranked "Größte Ladung zuerst", filterable by kind,
click-to-seek, with the uncalibrated and artefact caveats shown rather than hidden.

## What is wrong or unproven

Listed plainly, because the numbers above look more authoritative than they are.

**None of this is validated against labels.** Every figure is a self-consistency check. There is
no ground truth, so precision and recall are unknown. Stage 6 (evaluation harness) is what fixes
this, and it is blocked on stage 5 (labelling), which is blocked on nothing but work.

**The A-unit is a fallback.** 0.060 LP on the reference recording, derived from the 75th
percentile of fall amplitudes rather than from a Dosendruck. Every `amplitude_a` is therefore
indicative, and cross-kind ranking — a BE against an A — is exactly the comparison the manual says
requires the sensitivity calibration we do not have.

**No charge zone is named.** The corpus reads LP 4.45–5.97, which without a solo-electrode offset
would print as "Kampfzone" for 54 minutes. Supplying `lp_offset` on the analyse request fixes it;
there is no UI for that yet.

**`BE_MAX_RISE_TIME_SEC = 5.0` is a guess.** The manual never quantifies "schnell". The sensitivity
across the three sessions is tabulated in `detectors/discharge.py`. The count roughly doubles
between 3 s and 5 s and plateaus after, which is why 5.0 was chosen, but that is an argument from
the shape of the distribution, not from labels.

**Only 29 of 303 turns matched a cue.** Better than the 13 that exact matching found, but the
transcript clearly contains more instructions than that — turns like "Okay, beschreib." are real
cues too short to match safely without inviting false positives.

**Transcript word count drifted.** 1 576 words this run against 1 589 previously documented. Under
1%, and far inside the 1 576–3 234 spread that the `temperature=0.0` fix eliminated, but the
earlier claim of run-to-run identity does not hold across days and machine states.

**Summaries are untouched.** `§4` of the narrative design — phenomenon-aware prompts, topic
rollup, the automatic Sitzungsbericht — is not implemented. Summaries still use the single neutral
prompt and the legacy event list.

**`SN` and `FN` are not detected.** They are in the enum and the design, but needle-state
discrimination is L3 work that needs labels.

**The desktop bundle has not been rebuilt** since `services/phenomena/` and `services/protocol.py`
were added. `./scripts/build_desktop.sh` then `./scripts/smoke_desktop.sh` would confirm the new
packages import cleanly when frozen.
